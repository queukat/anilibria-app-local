2) Fixes by tasks A-E
A) Lifecycle-safe Flow collection in TV UI
- ProfileFragment: replaced launchIn(viewLifecycleOwner.lifecycleScope) collector with subscribeTo(...) helper (repeatOnLifecycle STARTED under the hood).
- WatchingFavoritesGridFragment: replaced manual lifecycleScope.launch { collect } for dialogRequests with subscribeTo(...).
- GradientBackgroundManager: replaced launchIn(activity.lifecycleScope) with launchInStarted(activity).
- Result: UI collectors are tied to STARTED lifecycle and do not keep collecting after view destruction.

B) MutableStateFlow/MutableSharedFlow encapsulation in TV ViewModels
- Mandatory files fixed: WatchingFavoritesViewModel, SearchViewModel, BaseCardsViewModel, DetailHeaderViewModel, ProfileViewModel.
- Also fixed analogous ViewModels found by search (Configuring, MainPages, AuthOtp, Player, PlayerEpisodes, PlayerSpeed, PlayerQuality, Schedule, AuthCredentials, SuggestionsRows, SuggestionsResult, BaseSearchValues, SearchForm, SearchCompleted, SearchSort, plus dependent usages in DetailRelated/MainSchedule/WatchingContinue).
- Pattern applied: private/protected mutable backing field + public StateFlow/SharedFlow via asStateFlow/asSharedFlow.
